[ OUR STORY ]

BUILT FROM INSIDE
THE TREATMENT
ROOM.

Before Lucci Media, there was the treatment room. With 10+ years of industry experience as a licensed esthetician and professional makeup artist, Lex Luciano went on to build a creator career working with globally recognized brands. She saw the same gap from both sides: beautiful businesses with incredible results, represented online by content that looked nothing like the experience inside.

She founded Lucci Media in December 2023 to close that gap. Since then, 350+ brands have trusted us with how they show up, from med spas and injectors to skincare lines, salons and lifestyle labels across New England and beyond.
