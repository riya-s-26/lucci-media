WELCOME TO THE TSA WORLD

Based in New York City, we are a content-first creative agency specializing in social media content production and management for beauty, fashion, jewelry, and lifestyle brands. Our team brings together creative strategy, production expertise, and platform knowledge under one roof, so that everything we create, from a single shoot day to a full management retainer, is built around protecting and elevating how your brand is perceived online.

We are not here to fill a grid. We are here to make sure your social presence reflects the level of intention your brand already has.

LED BY

Anna Sjalin

Anna founded The Social Agency with a clear mission: to help luxury e-commerce brands show up on social with the sophistication their products deserve. With a BA in Marketing and an MA in Organizational Communications and Business Administration from Copenhagen Business School, she's built her approach on understanding consumer behavior, brand positioning, and the strategic nuance luxury brands require.

Over five years, she's translated that academic foundation into scroll-stopping content and conversion-focused strategy for beauty, fashion, and lifestyle brands that refuse to blend in. Her expertise lies in recognizing that luxury marketing demands a different approach, and her personal passion for the industry ensures every brand she works with receives strategy that elevates rather than dilutes their positioning.
