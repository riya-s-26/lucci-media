MOST ASKED QUESTIONS

GENERAL FAQ

What services does Lucci Media offer?

Lucci Media specializes in social media management, content creation, branding strategy, and high-quality video production. We help businesses build a strong online presence, create engaging content, and grow their audience.

Who do you work with?

We primarily work with beauty, wellness, and lifestyle brands, but we’re open to working with businesses in other industries that align with our expertise.

Do you offer one-time content creation services, or is it only monthly management?

Yes! We offer one-time content days, where we capture high-quality video and photo content for your brand. We also provide ongoing social media management for businesses that need consistent content and strategy.

How do I know which package is right for me?

It depends on your business goals! If you need full social media management, our monthly packages are ideal. If you just need content for your team to post, our content creation services may be a better fit. Book a consultation, and we’ll help you choose the best option.

What’s the difference between content creation and social media management?

Content creation involves filming, editing, and providing high-quality visuals for your brand. Social media management includes content creation plus strategy, scheduling, posting, engagement, and growth efforts.
