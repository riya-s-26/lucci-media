Our Headquarters

Every Lucci Media shoot starts at Social Haus, our 3,000 sq ft creative studio in Lawrence, MA.

This is where we plan, shoot and build every campaign. A full cyc wall, multiple sets, and huge windows that pour in natural light all day. When a shot calls for something moodier, blackout curtains give us total control over the lighting.

There's a private dressing room and a dedicated makeup station so you're camera ready without leaving the building. Furniture moves, sets change, and the space shifts with whatever the concept needs.

Everything a creative campaign could ask for, under one roof.
