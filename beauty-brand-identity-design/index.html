BRAND IDENTITY

BRAND IDENTITY
AND LOGO DESIGN
FOR BEAUTY BUSINESSES

The brand world comes first. Everything else gets easier after it.

Before the photoshoot, before the website, there's the brand. Lucci Media designs brand identities for med spas, injectors, salons, estheticians and beauty founders who are opening, rebranding or finally ready to look as premium as their work. After 350+ brands, building that brand world is still the first thing we do.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Three logo variations: primary, secondary and submark

A custom color palette

Typography pairings for your website, social and print

Brand mockups showing your identity on signage, social and packaging

A brand guide so your team and vendors stay consistent

WHO IT'S FOR

Injectors and estheticians launching under a new name, med spas opening a second location, salons rebranding, and any business whose logo no longer matches its vision.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01 Discovery. We learn your clients, your price point and the feeling you want people to have.
  2. 02 Direction. You approve a moodboard and creative direction.
  3. 03 Design. Logo variations, palette and typography.
  4. 04 Delivery. Final files in every format, plus your brand guide.

INVESTMENT

Every brand identity is custom quoted. Share your vision in our inquiry form and we'll send a proposal.

Our recommended order: brand kit, then brand photography, then website. Each one builds on the last.
